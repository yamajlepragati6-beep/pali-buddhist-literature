1. Origin of Pali

Pali belongs to the Middle Indo-Aryan language group. It developed from the linguistic traditions of ancient India. It became closely associated with the preservation of Buddhist teachings.

2. Pali During the Time of Buddha

Gautama Buddha taught in languages understood by ordinary people of his time. His teachings were transmitted orally by his followers and later preserved in Buddhist literary traditions.

3. Development of Pali Literature

Buddhist monks played an important role in preserving Pali teachings. Over time, these teachings were collected and organized into important Buddhist texts.

4. Spread of Pali

Pali Buddhist literature spread beyond India, especially to Sri Lanka and other parts of Southeast Asia. It became an important language of the Theravada Buddhist tradition.

5. Historical Importance

Pali literature provides valuable information about ancient Indian society, philosophy, ethics, education and religious traditions. It continues to be studied around the world.
