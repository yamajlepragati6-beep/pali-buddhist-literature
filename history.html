1. Origin of Pali

Pali is a Middle Indo-Aryan language that developed in ancient India. It became an important language for preserving Buddhist teachings.

2. Pali During the Time of Buddha

Gautama Buddha taught in languages understood by ordinary people. His teachings were passed down orally by his followers.

3. Development of Pali Literature

Buddhist monks preserved and organized Buddha's teachings into important Buddhist texts.

4. Spread of Pali

Pali literature spread to Sri Lanka and Southeast Asia, becoming an important language of Theravada Buddhism.

5. Historical Importance

Pali literature provides valuable knowledge about ancient Indian society, philosophy, ethics and religious traditions.
